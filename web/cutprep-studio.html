Upload an image (PNG/JPG) or a vector file (SVG/DXF). CutPrep traces it, finds open contours and drop-out islands (like a pupil), holds them with a tab or bridge sized to your material, and exports a cut-ready DXF and SVG — scaled to real millimetres, tuned to your cutting method.
